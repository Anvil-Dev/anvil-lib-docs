# AnvilLib for TeaCon 2026

> AnvilLib Incentive Program - Prizes are 3D-printed souvenirs handmade by the author, with no commercial affiliation
> with TeaCon.

> In the name of the anvil, forge your modding legend.

On the stage of TeaCon 2026, AnvilLib aspires to be the most dependable hammer in your hand.

To encourage developers to try, dive deep into, and even give back to this toolchain, we have established three
independent awards.

Whether you are a newcomer just getting started or have built a stunning creation with AnvilLib, there is a place of
honor here for you.

## Forging Notes - Pioneer Award

> "A note may be small, but it records the starting point."

| Item       | Details                                                                 |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Purpose    | Commemorate participation, salute exploration                           |
| Criteria   | Entry successfully uses AnvilLib and completes TeaCon 2026 registration |
| Prize      | AnvilLib 3D-printed keychain                                            |
| Dimensions | 30mm x 25mm                                                             |
| Quantity   | Unlimited; all eligible participants receive one                        |

## Forged Tome - Excellence Award

> "Quench inspiration, bind code into a tome."

| Item       | Details                                                                        |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Purpose    | Highest honor, salute excellence                                               |
| Criteria   | Entry using AnvilLib places in the top three of any official TeaCon 2026 award |
| Prize      | AnvilLib 3D-printed honorary trophy                                            |
| Dimensions | 120mm x 100mm                                                                  |
| Quantity   | Determined by actual winners                                                   |

## Passing the Flame - Contribution Award

> "The sound of forging travels farther when shared."

| Item       | Details                                                                                                |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Purpose    | Community spirit, salute sharing                                                                       |
| Criteria   | Contribute code to AnvilLib, write tutorials, create example mods, or actively help other participants |
| Prize      | AnvilLib 3D-printed commemorative medal                                                                |
| Dimensions | 60mm x 50mm                                                                                            |
| Quantity   | Awarded at the discretion of the AnvilLib author                                                       |

## FAQ

**Q: Can I win multiple awards at the same time?**
A: Yes. "Forging Notes" is the baseline reward for all users; if your entry places in the TeaCon top three, you will
additionally receive the "Forged Tome" trophy; "Passing the Flame" is independently evaluated by us. The three do not
conflict with each other.

**Q: Do I need to pay for shipping?**
A: Shipping is covered by us. Winners only need to provide a delivery address.

**Q: I am not in China. Can I participate?**
A: Yes, but international shipping may take longer. Please understand. We will communicate with you individually.
